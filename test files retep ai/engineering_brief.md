# Northstar engineering brief

The main controller is an ESP32-S3.

## Sensors

- Air temperature: SHT45
- Barometric pressure: BMP390
- Wind speed: ultrasonic anemometer
- Rainfall: tipping-bucket gauge

The enclosure is rated IP67.

During bench testing, the engineering team decided that any internal temperature above 70 C should trigger thermal protection.

The prototype is nicknamed Kestrel.
