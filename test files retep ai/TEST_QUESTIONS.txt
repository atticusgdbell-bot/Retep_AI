RETEP TEST PACK - QUESTIONS WITH ANSWERS

1. Who is the project lead?
Answer: Mira Chen.

2. Where is Northstar being deployed?
Answer: Cape Scott, British Columbia.

3. What is the prototype nicknamed?
Answer: Kestrel.

4. What happened when the enclosure reached 71.2 C?
Answer: Thermal protection activated, and the radio transmitter was disabled.

5. What does error R17 mean?
Answer: R17 indicates that thermal protection was activated.

6. How many satellite modems are in inventory?
Answer: 5.

7. Where is the staging warehouse?
Answer: Nanaimo, British Columbia.

8. What is the thermal shutdown threshold?
Answer: 70 C.

9. How long should the backup battery last?
Answer: 18 hours.

10. Who handles logistics?
Answer: Tomas Reyes.

11. What is the approved budget?
Answer: CAD 84,500.

12. What generator model is mentioned in the legacy field note?
Answer: G-240.

13. Which files together tell you that Kestrel is going to Cape Scott and has an 18-hour battery target?
Answer: engineering_brief.md identifies the prototype as Kestrel. northstar_notes.txt and northstar_budget_report.pdf identify Cape Scott as the deployment site and state the 18-hour backup-battery target. northstar_config.yaml also identifies Cape Scott and Kestrel.

14. Compare the warning threshold in the Python file with the shutdown threshold in the engineering brief.
Answer: thresholds.py sets the thermal warning threshold at 65 C and the thermal shutdown threshold at 70 C. engineering_brief.md says temperatures above 70 C should trigger thermal protection. The warning threshold is therefore 5 C below the shutdown threshold.

15. What sensors are used by Northstar?
Answer: SHT45 for air temperature, BMP390 for barometric pressure, an ultrasonic anemometer for wind speed, and a tipping-bucket gauge for rainfall.

16. How often does the satellite uplink check in?
Answer: Every 15 minutes.

17. What happened after the enclosure cooled to 63.8 C?
Answer: Thermal protection was cleared.

18. Where is the backup generator stored?
Answer: At the Nanaimo staging warehouse.

19. Who is the field lead and who is the logistics lead?
Answer: Mira Chen is the field lead, and Tomas Reyes is the logistics lead.

20. What is the battery-low threshold in the Python configuration?
Answer: 20 percent.
